# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Anđela (Serbian, primary cycle tracker user, diary writer) and Barry (Chinese, partner, shared diary participant, Chinese learner). They use this app daily across desktop and mobile (PWA) in a long-distance relationship (Beijing ↔ Kikinda, 7000km apart).

## Product Purpose

Anđelin Ciklus is a personal cycle tracker and cross-cultural love diary for a long-distance couple. It tracks menstrual cycles with predictions, logs symptoms and moods, provides a shared diary with letter exchange, teaches Chinese language and culture, and celebrates their relationship with romantic touches (virtual garden, hug exchange, love notes, joint sun counter). Success looks like daily engagement from both partners.

## Brand Personality

Warm, romantic, intimate, cross-cultural. Think handwritten love letters, silk textures, candlelight. Feminine without being saccharine — grounded in real warmth. Three words: **tender, cultivated, heartfelt**.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS dashboards (gray tables, sidebar menus, data-heavy)
- Clinical health apps (sterile white, medical charts)
- Overdesigned "modern" UI with floating action buttons everywhere
- Purple gradients or generic Inter font

## Design Principles

1. **Warmth first** — every surface should feel like a personal letter, not a dashboard
2. **Cross-cultural integrity** — Chinese and Serbian elements coexist authentically, not as decoration
3. **Intimate scale** — designed for two people, not millions; every pixel says "this is for us"
4. **Restrained romance** — romantic touches that earn their place (garden, stars, tea room) rather than Valentine's Day on every screen
5. **Functional delight** — the cycle tracking is serious; the presentation can be playful

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Supports WCAG AA contrast minimums
- Reduced motion support via `prefers-reduced-motion`
- Dual-language UI (sr/zh-CN/en) switches instantly
- Supports dark mode
- Touch targets ≥44px
